- When can NISAR confirm a rate? (src/nisar_when.py, data/processed/nisar_confirm.json). Model: independent error on every acquisition date (lag-1 correlation of chain increments measured -0.47 at Willcox and -0.51 at Eloy; the model predicts -0.5). Averaging pixels does not help because a date error is common to the whole scene. Willcox per-date error calibrated to 14.8 mm so the model reproduces the measured OLS standard error (0.93 cm/yr); Eloy 6.7 mm from only 10 dates (weak). Years of continuous 12-day data for a 90% chance of reaching 3 sigma: Willcox basin (S1 rate 2.7 cm/yr) 1.27 yr, about Feb 2027 if there are no gaps; the simulated chance at our current 0.99 yr is only 50%, which matches the unstable rate we saw. Eloy (1.3 cm/yr) 1.21 yr from the Jun 19 2026 start, about Sep 2027. Generic: 1 cm/yr needs 1.5 to 2.5 yr, 0.5 cm/yr needs 2.3 to 4 yr. Covers random per-date error only; reference drift, seasonal motion and LOS-vs-vertical are not included. north_willcox, south and east entries in the file borrow Willcox noise because NISAR was not processed there: do NOT quote them on the site.

- NISAR GCOV (backscatter) at Willcox (src/gcov_remote.py, gcov_extract.py, gcov_farmland.py; data/processed/gcov_farmland.json; docs/gcov_farmland_willcox.png).   - Cropland = 2.1% of the box (12,615 acres) but 7.4% of the Sentinel-1 sinking zone (8,105 acres), 3.6 times its share.
  - Sentinel-1 rate, cropland minus desert shrubland: -2.31 cm/yr (95% CI -3.22 to -1.61, 2 km tile bootstrap); 67% of cropland cells vs 16% of desert cells at or below -1 cm/yr. CAUTION: C-band InSAR over crops can carry an apparent-subsidence bias (published: 2-4 cm/yr for cotton, orchards worst vs L-band; several mm/yr in general), so part of this may not be real. NISAR L-band check: -5.0 cm/yr but only 405 cropland cells coherent all year, CI -5.8 to +0.5: same sign, size not trusted.

  - Not significant, kept off the site: cropland cells with a large yearly HV swing sink -1.06 cm/yr faster than other cropland (CI -2.25 to +0.44).
  - Maturity: our GCOV dates mix Beta (Oct 26 2025), Provisional (most) and Validated/urgent-response (Nov 2 and Nov 14 2026, CRID P05036). The Nov 2 brightening (+3.1 dB) follows 29 mm of rain in 3 days, so it is weather, not a calibration step; a small calibration step cannot be ruled out.

# AI assistance disclosure

Per NASA Space Apps rules, this project discloses its use of AI ("Super Intelligence") tools.

An AI coding assistant was used to help with:
- Structuring the project brief and documentation.
- Pair-programming the web map front end (`web/`): the study-area loader, the
  time-series charts and hover interaction, the honest "pending" and
  "preliminary" data states, and layout/styling.
- Drafting plain-language explainer and limits text.

All measured values come from the processing pipeline in `src/` run by the team
on the real NISAR and Sentinel-1 data. No rates, dates or displacements were
generated by an AI tool. Team members reviewed and ran all code, and made the
scientific and design decisions.

No NASA branding or mission identifiers were generated by AI tools.

SI-generated image: the site logo was generated with ChatGPT (OpenAI) from the team's own design brief and then edited by the team (background removed, resized). As the Space Apps rules require, it carries a visible label on the site: an "AI" mark on the header logo, an "AI-generated logo" badge on the large logo, and a note in the footer. Favicons and app icons are too small to label and use the same image.

Phone backdrop: the soft desert-sunset silhouette behind the opening text on the phone Overview page (`web/assets/desert-backdrop.webp`) was made from a photograph supplied by the team, using `src/make_backdrop.py` (crop, find the sky, tint the land, wash the sky). The photograph itself is not in the repository. The script was written with an AI coding assistant; the image is a photographic silhouette, not an AI-generated picture. The page labels it "silhouette traced from a photograph". Photograph source and licence: to be confirmed by the team (see docs/DATA_AND_LICENSES.md).
